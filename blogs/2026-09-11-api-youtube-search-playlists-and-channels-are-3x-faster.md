YouTube discovery is faster across the board. Keyword search, playlist listings and channel listings now return in well under a second at the median, down from about 2 seconds, and the slow tail is gone: 90th-percentile latency dropped from 3 to 5 seconds to around 1.2 seconds. Long playlists and channels benefit most.
