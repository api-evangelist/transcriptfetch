You can now add TranscriptFetch straight from Claude's connector directory: search for TranscriptFetch, click Connect, and sign in. There's no server URL to paste. Once connected, Claude can get the transcript of a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram video, search for videos, and list channels, profiles and playlists, with AI transcription when a video has no captions.
