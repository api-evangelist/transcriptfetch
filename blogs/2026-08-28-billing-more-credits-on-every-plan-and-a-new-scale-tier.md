Basic now includes 1,000 credits a month at the same $5 (up from 500), Pro includes 10,000 at $15 (up from 5,000), Mega stays at $45 for 50,000, and a new Scale plan offers 300,000 credits for $229. Top-ups are also cheaper: instead of a flat $10 per 1,000, they now match your plan's own rate — $5 per 1,000 on Basic, $1.50 on Pro, $0.90 on Mega, $0.76 on Scale. Already subscribed?
