New accounts now start with 50 free credits instead of 100, and accounts without a plan refresh to 50 credits each month. Everything else about the free tier is unchanged: no card required, 1 credit per successful transcript or listing page, 1 credit per minute of AI transcription, and failures never cost anything. Fifty credits is still enough to integrate and test every endpoint.
